One feature over 1.4.1, and two fixes that came with it.

- While a steep is running, the short break, the hydration reminder and the long break all wait for it.
  Nothing is announced and nothing is stacked on top of the brewing countdown. A steep is short, so
  holding a break for the sake of a countdown that is nearly over costs nothing.
- Once the steep has more than two and a half minutes left it can hold nothing further. That is the
  herbal schedule, whose steeps run five to eight minutes: there the app wins, the break comes as it
  should, and the brewing bubble is put away until the break is over. The session is never dropped.
- The hydration reminder is no longer lost. It used to vanish unheard whenever the single reminder slot
  was held by the tea, and the interval restarted as though nothing had happened.
